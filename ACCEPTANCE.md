# Acceptance requirements

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. This catalogue covers every Latchkey target and push-further clause. **Two clauses are done, three are partial and 71 are missing**. LKY-001 proves its client-held foundation and LKY-006 its current sharing doors; the partial rows retain their specific unproved points. Runtime, Records command/restore and 1440/390 browser evidence is mapped below. Exact-candidate CI run IDs are recorded in the campaign plan. No live-native or independent-review result is claimed. A journey must pass through the actual product; a document parser check does not establish working behavior.

Use the acceptance conventions in CONTRACT.md: realistic US business data, 1440 and 390 browser journeys, real native clients, keyboard and assistive technology, and fakes only at paid or network edges using official schemas. Each journey inherits live tenant, role and version checks, audit without secrets, inactive archive restore, erasure and replay behavior and human approval. Provider sandbox evidence and live-provider proof are labeled distinctly. Security design, third-party assessment and certification require actual documents. Do not weaken an assertion or replace a client with mock app code to pass. CI is the full wall; local work runs only meaningful checks for changed behavior.
