# Latchkey product contract

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Latchkey is the complete business password and secrets manager a US company buys instead of 1Password Business, Bitwarden, Keeper or LastPass Business, with the collaboration, privacy and developer depth buyers seek from Passbolt, Proton Pass for Business and Infisical. Dashlane Omnix, NordPass Business and Psono are included in the switching destination. It keeps employee logins, TOTP and passkeys, shared privileged credentials and machine secrets encrypted, usable on every device and accountable through grant, use, offboarding and verified rotation.

This is a build target, not a claim of shipped capability. No repository or code was available for judgment when the draft was written. All clauses are missing, including the original desk IDs LKY-001–030, which retain their meaning and numbering. Difficulty in encryption, provider apps, native clients, controlled browsers, certification or migration is engineering work in the named clauses, not a scope reduction. Naming and the friendly latchkey mark remain as specified by the desk; this document changes no brand, UI or homepage.

A person approves what matters; agents carry permitted remediation through to observed results. Auth owns workforce identity and SSO into applications. Latchkey owns the credentials and secret lifecycles that SSO cannot replace.

## Acceptance conventions

Every clause below names buyer behavior and a journey through a public browser, installed client, CLI, API or provider door. Browser journeys include realistic US-company data, success, refusal, empty, loading, expiry and failure states at **1440 and 390 CSS pixels**; native mobile, autofill and client requirements additionally use their actual platform APIs. Keyboard and assistive-technology operation is required. These are future acceptance obligations, not screenshots or tests claimed to exist today.

Use the actual migrated product and dedicated synthetic and test accounts. Fakes belong only at paid or network provider edges and follow the provider's official published schema and protocol; preserve request scope, signature, ordering, pagination, rate-limit and failure behavior. Such a fake establishes bounded adapter behavior, not a live connection. Native clients, end-to-end encryption, fill-only isolation, actual source exports and real-page fill coverage cannot be replaced by mock application code. Security review and certification clauses require their actual independent documents. Every journey includes denial of cross-tenant, expired and revoked authority, no secret leakage, a durable attributed result and the archive and erasure consequences of LKY-033 and LKY-034. No agent approves a person's decision.

Routes and command names describe required public doors and may be made concrete by implementation without changing the promised behavior. One-off fixes are shown by before and after evidence; permanent tests protect meaningful flows rather than pinning copy or document hashes.

## Target clauses


### Foundation and owned operation

**LKY-031 — Owner-run installation through Base.** A buyer installs the complete Latchkey service in their own Cloudflare account through Base, chooses its domain and initial owner, and controls storage, DNS, deployment credentials, integrations and USD infrastructure costs. Setup verifies required bindings, email, regional placement and encryption configuration before inviting members. The same credential, admin, machine and MSP capabilities operate in an owner-run installation as in any managed edition. Desktop clients, extensions, native apps, trusted brokers and private-network connectors are real deliverables, not reasons to reduce the target to a web demo. Settings names each component, owner and readiness state; upgrades preserve vault data, compatible clients, pending work and audit. A failed setup or migration cannot create an apparently usable insecure vault. The public Lobby and /contract page contain no customer metadata or vault data.

**Acceptance evidence:** From a clean Cloudflare account use the Base installation command and setup browser to provision a US installation, invite an IT owner and open the vault through the configured domain. Break a binding and regional prerequisite and require a visible failed prerequisite with no invite or credential use. Upgrade a populated installation and compare items, grants, running-operation states and audit. Visit Lobby and /contract signed out and confirm no tenant data.

**LKY-032 — Sign-in and server-side authority.** Sign-in identifies a person independently of decrypting their vault. Native sign-in, Auth and direct Google Workspace, Entra ID and Okta SSO are supported; SAML and OIDC authentication never gives the identity provider a vault decryption key. Owners, security administrators, recovery administrators, vault managers, members, guests, auditors, billing administrators, MSP operators and machine identities have distinct least-privilege rights. Every route, API, attachment, search result, export, job and broker operation checks the current tenant, identity and effective object rights; UI hiding is insufficient. Browser mutations check origin and request integrity. Sessions expire, rotate and can be revoked, and setup has no default or shared password. A signed-out or foreign request cannot enumerate item names, membership, existence or installation settings.

**Acceptance evidence:** Use native and each named SSO sign-in against provider sandboxes or official SAML or OIDC-schema fixtures, then attempt direct item, attachment, API and admin reads and writes as guest, auditor, billing admin, another tenant and signed-out caller. Refuse every unauthorized path without side effects. Replay a revoked session and a cross-origin mutation, and show that a valid SSO login on an untrusted device still cannot decrypt.

**LKY-033 — Whole-product encrypted archive and inactive restore.** The owner exports and restores versioned, documented, integrity-checked archives containing encrypted vault item values, all item types and attachments, histories, tags, ownership, grants as historical definitions, secret projects and environments, reports, findings, review decisions, operation history and audit. Credential values are core product data and must be portable; an archive of empty definitions is insufficient. Export is encrypted by a buyer-held export key and policy and approval gated, with a client-side readable open-format option under LKY-017. Client-held keys are rewrapped for explicitly approved destination recipients. Installation sessions, active machine and agent tokens, device trust, connector authorization and approval capabilities do not travel. Restored historical grants do not authorize new access; schedules, scans, rotations, webhooks and sends stay inactive until separately reviewed. Corrupt or nonempty-ineligible restore is atomic, interruption is resumable, and later erasure journals fence deleted objects. An old archive without a later journal cannot prove subsequent erasure and says so.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through Base Records and the documented CLI export a populated company and personal-account separation, restore to a fresh installation controlled by a different owner, and re-export to compare every domain, attachment digest and history. Demonstrate destination key rewrapping by approved people and absence of active installation authority. Interrupt and resume, corrupt a chunk and try an ineligible receiving state; no partial product writes or external effects occur. Apply a later erasure journal and show erased items stay absent.

**LKY-034 — Erasure with anonymized audit and replay fencing.** Archiving is reversible; explicit erasure is a separate confirmed action by an authorized person. Within five minutes erased item and person content, attachments, indexes, finding snippets, model contexts, cached server derivatives and pending notifications disappear from all public reads and future exports or agent requests. Retain only non-identifying tamper-evident audit facts, counts and keyed replay fences that cannot reconstruct the original secret or identity. Retention holds are explicit and do not silently relabel erasure as complete. Connected clients receive deletion and key invalidation at their next sync, and bounded leases limit disconnected caches under LKY-038. Source-system content, external copies, provider backups and values already copied by authorized recipients cannot be recalled: show their deletion and rotation requests and verified or pending results separately. Backup expiry and erasure-journal restore behavior are documented and exercised.

**Acceptance evidence:** Erase a member and an exposed secret through the UI and API; after five minutes search, export, attachments, health reports, pending jobs, MCP and model-request capture contain no original data, while anonymized audit verifies. Replay source scan events, a queued notification and an old import, and restore an earlier archive with the erasure journal: nothing recreates erased originals. Reconnect a formerly offline client and show key invalidation and the separate status of external deletion requests.

**LKY-035 — Agents are scoped credentials with human approval.** A person delegates a revocable credential bound to installation, tenant, sponsoring person, purpose, exact resources, allowed actions, provider targets, time and optional use limit. Agents are not administrator identities, cannot inherit the owner's browser session, issue subdelegations or approve their own access. Sensitive grants, shared privileged reveals, business-critical rotations, recovery-admin changes and all data exports require a named authorized human to review the exact action. The approval binds resource and version, target, purpose, consequences and expiry; changed inputs require new approval. Previously approved low-risk rotation schedules may run only within their recorded bounds. Model output, imported text, chat instructions and provider responses carry no authority. Audit records requester, sponsoring person, approver, credential and external result without the secret.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through the delegation API and MCP create a read-posture grant and try item reveal, export, a foreign target, expired use, self-approval and scope expansion via injected source text. Refuse each. Approve one exact critical rotation on the phone, mutate the item or provider target before execution and require fresh approval. Revoke a grant during a running operation and stop the next privileged step while retaining the actual prior effects.

**LKY-036 — US-first operation and explicit regional controls.** The first buyer is a US company of 10–1,000 people; prices, plan comparisons, billing records and budgets default to USD, language to English, and reports and reminders to the buyer's selected US timezone with daylight-saving behavior. US residency is the default for encrypted data, backups, logs and approved secret-processing brokers; setup proves provider placement and identifies any subcontractor or notification and model destination that would cross that boundary before enabling it. A later EU edition is a complete deployment option rather than an implicit migration. Cryptographic and authenticator design follows NIST SP 800-63B and CISA phishing-resistant MFA guidance; healthcare buyers get HIPAA-ready controls and a documented BAA path. Region, compliance and certification claims name their actual scope and never appear as awarded before evidence exists.

**Acceptance evidence:** Install using US defaults and inspect placement for database, objects, backups, audit, broker and agent requests. Refuse an unapproved out-of-region destination. Schedule US Eastern and Pacific reminders across spring and fall DST and compare exports with UTC audit timestamps. Display USD estimates and the BAA request and control package; the public security page distinguishes implemented controls, review evidence and unearned certification.

**LKY-056 — Recover the vault service without losing the keys.** Owners can back up encrypted state and prove recovery without using the password manager to retrieve the only credentials needed to restore that same password manager. A documented offline recovery kit, multiple named owners, owner-held archive keys and separately protected Cloudflare and domain access remove that circular dependency. Health distinguishes sync, notification, broker and connector reachability from vault content health; a provider outage cannot be described as healthy coverage. Backups, restoration rehearsal, schema compatibility and compromised-device or signing-key replacement have accountable runbooks. Existing authorized offline clients remain usable within policy during a service outage; new grants and approvals never succeed from stale cached policy. Storage and job limits are visible before action, with no silent truncation at the target 1,000-person scale.

**Acceptance evidence:** From the documented recovery kit on a clean machine restore after losing the control-plane account session and primary admin device, without reading a key from the unavailable vault. Compare item, history and attachment totals at a 1,000-member installation. Stop sync, broker, notification and connector components independently and inspect distinct unavailable or stale states. Restore inactive and verify no rotation, send or grant resumes without fresh authority.


### Vault and everyday clients

**LKY-001 — Zero-knowledge vault and published security design.** Every item, secret value, TOTP seed, passkey private material, custom field, attachment and sensitive item metadata is encrypted on the client with per-vault AES-256-GCM keys and authenticated context. Each recipient gets only wrapped keys authorized for that vault; their account key derives from the unlock secret plus a device-held secret key. Password-authenticated sign-in uses a reviewed SRP or OPAQUE exchange, so the server never receives the password or an equivalent decryption secret. Key derivation, nonce generation, device enrollment, membership removal, recovery, SSO and rotation are specified in a published design. The operator and vendor staff cannot recover plaintext from server state, backups or logs. Owner-authorized clients, scanners and brokers decrypt only inside the explicitly trusted client boundary; they are not a vendor key escrow. The threat model describes malicious client and update and endpoint compromise honestly.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through signup, create, share, edit, export and recovery and machine-runtime doors put unique synthetic secrets in every item type; inspect full server database dumps, backups, logs, error reports and network payloads for plaintext and derivable keys. Demonstrate authenticated-ciphertext and context tampering rejection and key separation across tenants. Publish the protocol and design and obtain an independent cryptographic review covering recovery, SSO, trusted broker and malicious-server and update boundaries; review findings have resolved or explicitly open dispositions.

**LKY-002 — Complete item types, generator and history.** Members create logins with multiple URL rules, TOTP and passkeys; secure notes, cards, identities, bank accounts, SSH keys, API credentials, databases, servers, Wi-Fi, software licenses and documents with encrypted attachments. Each supports custom fields, labels, tags, favorites, ownership, searchable non-secret views and clear reveal, copy and fill affordances appropriate to its type. A cryptographically random password and passphrase generator follows group policy without weakening entropy for convenience; generated-history retention is encrypted and controllable. Version history includes field and attachment changes, author and time, with a preview and explicit restore that appends a new version. Restoring an old provider password warns that vault history does not revert the provider; verified rotation state is separate.

**Acceptance evidence:** Create, edit, fill and copy as appropriate and restore every named type through the vault client with custom fields and attachments. Change one item ten times and retrieve all ten versions, restoring an earlier version without deleting later history. Verify TOTP against standard vectors, passkey sign-in, generated-policy compliance, unauthorized history and attachment denial and unchanged provider rotation state after a history-only restore.

**LKY-003 — Everyday apps, autofill and passkeys.** Ship Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Brave extensions, macOS, Windows and Linux desktop clients, iOS and Android apps with system autofill, and the web vault. Support save-on-submit, update-existing prompts, multi-step logins, multiple accounts per origin, permitted iframes, TOTP, cards and identities, accessible keyboard selection and native app associations. Strict domain, origin and relying-party matching refuses lookalike, insecure or mismatched frame targets; an explicit URL exception is reviewed and never learned from a model suggestion. Passkeys can be created, stored, selected, used and synchronized across supported clients with provider-compatible cross-device authentication. Offline access inherits LKY-038. A user-reported fill failure can be diagnosed without uploading passwords or private page contents.

**Acceptance evidence:** Run a consented fill suite across at least 300 real sign-in pages using dedicated test accounts and each named browser and platform, including US bank, payroll, multi-step, iframe and CAPTCHA handoff cases. Publish failures individually; a fake login-page suite alone is insufficient. Verify save, update and TOTP, card and identity fields, domain lookalikes, frame-origin attacks and an unauthorized URL exception. Create a passkey on a phone and sign in from desktop, then complete web journeys at 1440/390 and native system-autofill journeys.

**LKY-004 — Unlock and device security.** Unlock uses password plus device secret key, supported biometric and system secure storage, a cryptographically capable passkey, or SSO through an approved trusted device. Authentication success alone never unwraps a new device's keys. An existing trusted device or designated recovery administrator approves authenticated key enrollment, with visible device identity and verification. Administrators set inactivity, background and restart locks, clipboard clearing and device revocation; applications use OS key stores and encrypted caches. Remote sign-out invalidates sessions and future key access, and locks and clears a reachable client. A locked stolen device's cached vault remains unreadable. No claim of remote erasure covers already decrypted memory, a compromised OS or previously copied secrets; LKY-010 and LKY-038 close residual exposure through expiry and rotation.

**Acceptance evidence:** Enroll a second phone through the actual app and device approval journey, reject a mismatched device challenge and show password and SSO alone on an untrusted device cannot decrypt. Exercise biometric, passkey and SSO unlock, timeout and clipboard clearing. Sign out a locked lost laptop remotely, attempt its old session and cache on reconnect and after lease expiry offline, and verify refusal while exposing the remaining copied-secret rotation obligations.

**LKY-017 — Open export and self-hosting parity.** An authorized person exports selected items, vaults or the entire company in a documented open format, encrypted by default, with a separately approved plaintext export where policy permits. Data and attachments are complete, counts and omissions are explicit, and exports can be reimported without loss. Ciphertext or key envelopes are usable by the buyer, not bound to a vendor account that can disappear. Owner-run and self-hosted installations receive the same client and product capabilities and security fixes as managed editions. Export, backup and decryption are not withheld because a subscription expired; cancellation and operational shutdown disclose consequences and provide an exit path. Vault product credentials remain portable even though active installation authority does not, as LKY-033 specifies.

**Acceptance evidence:** Export all item types, TOTP, histories and attachments, with a permitted plaintext selection and encrypted full archive; reimport into a fresh owner-run installation and compare content and digests and effective destination access. Deny an export-policy violation and require exact human approval. Repeat with an expired commercial entitlement and exercise cancellation; the buyer retains their encrypted archive and recovery path. Run the same product acceptance doors on owner-run and managed deployments.

**LKY-027 — Personal and family accounts remain separate.** A member can link a free personal or family account and switch between it and work without merging ownership or key domains. Administrators cannot search, report on, recover or export personal content through company authority. Work-item ownership policy makes its effect visible at creation and import; moving a personal item into company ownership requires the person's explicit informed action. When employment ends, company items and required hand-back remain with the company while personal items and personal account access remain with the person, including a clear path after company-sponsored benefits end. Family sharing, member management and emergency access do not inherit corporate administrators.

**Acceptance evidence:** Link work and family accounts on web, extension and phone, put different credentials in each and attempt cross-account search, export and recovery as a company admin. Offboard the member through the directory and verify company access ends and company items transfer, while personal and family access and data survive independently. Move a personal item to work only after an explicit ownership confirmation and compare the audit without disclosing personal data.

**LKY-037 — Find, organize, archive and restore work.** Search and saved filters cover permitted vaults, item type, tag, favorites, owner, expiry and health findings without leaking another vault's titles or plaintext to the server. Members can organize nested collections, bulk move, tag and archive permitted items, preview the exact affected objects and rights, and undo reversible operations. Archive removes an item from normal fill suggestions and active remediation without deleting history; trash and permanent erasure are distinct. A bulk move recomputes the entire resulting recipient set before approval and never broadens access silently. Archive and restore cannot revive a revoked share, approval, machine lease or old provider credential.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through search and bulk-action doors find a realistic 10,000-item company vault on desktop and phone, preview moving a mixed-permission selection, and refuse objects the caller cannot manage. Archive one credential, confirm it disappears from fill and active-work queues, restore it and retain history without restoring old links or grants. Try an unauthorized exact-title search and verify no result, count or timing-dependent existence disclosure in the product response.

**LKY-038 — Offline continuity, conflicts and bounded revocation.** Authorized clients retain encrypted local data for explicitly allowed vaults and can read and fill offline within an owner-set maximum offline lease, no longer than 15 minutes for company vault unlock authority; sensitive vaults may require an online check for every use. The UI shows last sync, remaining lease and unavailable actions. Disconnected clients cannot receive immediate revocation: after lease expiry work vaults lock until current authority is checked, and changing the device clock cannot extend access. Online suspension is enforced within one sync cycle, at most one minute; changes to group policy reach online clients within five minutes. Concurrent edits preserve both versions and require a visible resolution rather than last-write data loss. Queued audit and edits reconcile idempotently; high-risk grants, exports and rotation approvals require live policy.

**Acceptance evidence:** Disconnect two authorized clients, edit the same item, remove one member and rotate the shared password online. Verify old authority ends at the bounded lease even with clock rollback, and the reconnecting client cannot overwrite the new credential. Resolve a genuine concurrent permitted edit with both histories retained. Show stale or offline labels, refuse offline export or new sensitive approval, and replay queued events without duplicated history or silent gaps.

**LKY-039 — Rollout, accessibility and support without disclosure.** Admins pilot and deploy extensions and apps using managed-browser policy, Intune and Jamf tooling or downloadable signed installers, track invitations and enrollment and guide members from their existing browser manager. The first successful save, fill and recovery setup have short documented journeys; a policy conflict explains the responsible group and fix. Core web, admin and approval journeys work by keyboard and assistive technology at 1440 and 390, with clear focus, labels, contrast and non-color status. Native clients respect OS accessibility. Help, diagnostics and update and compatibility information are available during outages without submitting secrets. Diagnostic bundles show exactly what metadata leaves and require permission; no support worker can unlock a vault.

**Acceptance evidence:** Pilot with an IT admin, finance employee, contractor and developer; deploy supported clients through the named management tools or signed installers and complete invite, recovery enrollment, first save and fill and a policy refusal. Exercise keyboard and screen-reader paths and mobile approval, expired sharing, empty, error and offline states at 1440/390. Submit a redacted diagnostic bundle and inspect its outgoing contents, then try to use support authority to decrypt and require refusal.

**LKY-050 — Hide-my-email aliases with a real forwarding provider.** Members generate and save a unique email alias alongside a login, choose its approved forwarding mailbox and disable or replace an abused alias without losing the login. Business-owned aliases and forwarding policy remain company-owned; personal aliases remain personal. Use a real alias provider such as SimpleLogin or a buyer-controlled mail service through a documented adapter, showing domain and mailbox verification, current delivery state, subscription dependency and provider failure. Latchkey manages the credential and alias relationship and never turns into a general mail host or silently forwards work mail to a personal account.

**Acceptance evidence:** From a real signup form create an alias using the provider sandbox or official-schema network fixture, save it with a credential, receive a verification message at the permitted mailbox and sign in. Disable it and verify forwarding stops at the provider while the saved login remains. Deny an unauthorized forwarding change, separate company and personal alias export and show delivery failure without claiming mail arrived.


### Sharing, identity and administration

**LKY-005 — Private vaults, groups and effective permissions.** Every employee has a private work vault with explicit company and personal ownership rules; shared vaults and nested collections grant view, fill-only, edit and manage rights to people or groups. Effective-access preview shows every direct and inherited grant and the source policy. Membership removal rekeys future access and enforces current rights at every public door. Fill-only means the requester cannot reveal, copy, export or receive the credential value: an owner-authorized isolated fill broker delivers it to the approved provider, not the requester's ordinary DOM, devtools, clipboard or API response. If a target and client cannot supply that guarantee it refuses fill-only with a visible unsupported path until that integration is engineered; hiding a reveal button is never fulfillment. Plaintext already disclosed to a previously authorized viewer is an exposure to rotate.

**Acceptance evidence:** Create nested finance collections and overlapping groups; inspect effective rights as member, editor and manager. A fill-only member signs into an approved account via the controlled broker; inspect browser, network, clipboard, API, export and history and model logs for secret absence and an attributed fill event. Attempt an ordinary DOM fill under fill-only and require refusal. Remove the group and show all online devices lose access within LKY-038's bound; an old copied credential stays on the rotation list.

**LKY-006 — Internal, guest and one-time sharing.** Share items or vaults with named people and groups and constrained contractor guests, with least-privilege rights, optional approval and start and end times. External secret and file links work without an account, can require a separate password and verified recipient, expire automatically and support one-time or bounded views plus immediate revocation. Link capabilities never appear in analytics, referrers or audit plaintext; the encrypted payload and key-delivery design preserve LKY-001. A link preview and scanner does not consume a one-time view, while concurrent actual opens cannot exceed its limit. Changing an item or deleting its source does not silently extend an old grant. Recipients are told that revealed material cannot be recalled and must be rotated if exposure matters.

**Acceptance evidence:** Grant a contractor exactly two items until Friday and advance the clock: access ends without admin action across UI and API. Share a secret and file by protected one-time link, run link-preview GETs and simultaneous actual opens, and allow only one committed view. Revoke another link and verify cached page refresh and download cannot fetch it. Test wrong recipient and password, expiry and policy-blocked external sharing at 1440/390 without leaking capabilities.

**LKY-007 — Admin console, policies and travel mode.** Admins set per-group unlock and MFA, generator and password, recovery enrollment, export, sharing, work-item ownership, personal-vault, browser and device and network and country policies. Effective policy has deterministic conflict rules and explains its source to the member. Export restrictions cover web, CLI, clients, history and attachment paths; permission to use a secret is not permission to export. Approved travel mode excludes designated vaults from selected devices until the person returns and current policy reauthorizes them, with no residual decryptable cache. Admins preview affected users and devices before applying a material change and track convergence; an unreachable client stays visibly outstanding until its bounded lease ends. No policy can guarantee recall of secrets already revealed.

**Acceptance evidence:** Apply a group policy blocking export and requiring recovery and MFA, then exercise every export and client door within five minutes. Remove a group and inspect effective rights and sync status across devices. Enable travel mode, inspect the traveling device's encrypted cache and key access and show excluded vaults cannot decrypt offline; disable only under live authority. Submit conflicting policies and a forbidden device and network unlock and inspect deterministic, useful reasons.

**LKY-008 — Directory, SSO unlock and provisioning.** Support SCIM 2.0 from Auth, Okta, Entra ID, JumpCloud and OneLogin; Google Workspace uses its supported directory and provisioning mechanism or an explicitly documented bridge rather than claiming a generic outbound SCIM service. Directories include Active Directory and LDAP through a scoped connector. Groups map to vault grants after admin review; SAML and OIDC unlock uses trusted-device decryption and preserves recovery without vendor keys. Identity matching uses stable provider IDs and reviewed account linking, not a reused email address. Join, rename, suspend, reactivate and delete are idempotent and visible; suspension blocks online unlock and secret fetch within one sync cycle. Auth owns the workforce directory, MFA and SSO into other applications; Latchkey is its relying party and password and secrets system.

**Acceptance evidence:** In each named provider sandbox or official-schema fixture provision, rename, reassign groups, suspend, reactivate and delete a user; repeat and out-of-order events cannot add unauthorized access. Adding an Okta group gives only mapped vaults; suspending in Auth blocks online unlock and fetch within one minute and offline use follows LKY-038. Test a renamed or reused email and untrusted SSO device, and show neither obtains keys through identity matching alone.

**LKY-009 — Recover accounts without vendor access.** Members enroll self-recovery codes and administrator-assisted recovery with keys held by named recovery administrators; vendor and operator staff and server state alone cannot recover a vault. Recovery shows the target person, identity checks, affected vaults and devices, approvals and newly issued account keys, and notifies the member through a previously verified channel. Lost MFA and lost vault secret are distinct ceremonies; rotating one does not silently bypass the other. Personal emergency access names a contact, allowed scope and waiting period, with notices, cancellation and denial while the owner is able to respond. Recovery suspends old device and session authority, rewraps keys to the new owner-authorized key and retains an audit event without logging recovery material.

**Acceptance evidence:** On a clean client lose an enrolled member's unlock secret, verify their identity and complete recovery with designated admin-held keys; show the prior device and session no longer works and all retained items decrypt for the member. Attempt recovery using only operator and server access and require failure. Exercise self-recovery once, invalid or reused code and emergency-contact waiting, cancel and expiry paths without early access or plaintext in logs.

**LKY-010 — Offboarding and credential hand-back.** Auth, Willow, Linen or a connected directory leaver signal immediately suspends future online unlock and fetch and terminates agent and machine grants sponsored by the leaver unless an authorized owner explicitly reassigns service ownership. Company items in a private work vault transfer cryptographically to a named authorized custodian; a company ownership policy requires recovery and escrow enrollment before company items are created, so hand-back is possible without a vendor key. List all currently accessible shared credentials and historic reveal-capable grants or actual uses in the last 90 days, with distinct evidence and uncertainty. Every potentially exposed credential receives a tracked rotation obligation, owner and criticality. Personal vaults remain untouched. Replayed HR events cannot revive access, and future-dated leavers follow the source's authoritative date rather than a model inference.

**Acceptance evidence:** Offboard a finance employee with private company items, shared reveals, brokered fills, a service identity and separate family account. Within one minute produce suspension, hand-back inventory and exposure and rotation tasks; decrypt transferred company items as the custodian and refuse the leaver. Include a removed historic grant from the prior 90 days and an offline client gap. Replay and reorder events, then verify personal data survives and critical rotations await named human approval.

**LKY-025 — Consent-based unmanaged credential discovery.** With recorded organization policy and member consent, the extension detects company sign-ins not in the vault, offers import of browser-saved work credentials, identifies duplicate unmanaged accounts and reports apps suitable for SSO. Discovery is scoped to work domains and profiles and necessary metadata, with pause, exclusions and deletion; it never uploads personal browsing history, plaintext work passwords or a whole page to a model. Each finding offers a save-to-owner and team-vault action with permission preview. Admins can hand an SSO candidate to Auth, retaining source and confidence without pretending the app is already federated. Browser phishing-risk warnings complement strict fill origin checks; a detected sign-in is neither permission to harvest nor permission to grant access.

**Acceptance evidence:** Enable consent in a managed work profile, sign into a previously unknown SaaS tool and receive a scoped save proposal and admin SSO candidate. Decline it, exclude a site and repeat in a personal profile: no secret or history is uploaded or rediscovered outside consent. Save after reviewing destination rights and send a candidate to Auth; the app remains marked unmanaged until Auth reports the actual SSO outcome. Test a lookalike warning without collecting the entered password.

**LKY-026 — Device posture before vault unlock.** Read device encryption, supported OS version, screen lock and management status from Jamf, Kandji, Intune and Linen asset records, matching a device through a verified identifier. An admin's per-vault and group policy can refuse unhealthy, unmanaged or stale-posture devices before unwrap and fill. Show the source timestamp, required control and concrete fix, separating unknown or unreachable posture from healthy. Overrides have a named human, reason, duration and audit, and cannot become permanent through renewal by an agent. Endpoint enrollment, patching, inventory ownership and remote device management stay in Linen or the chosen MDM; Latchkey consumes their posture.

**Acceptance evidence:** Connect each named source via a sandbox or official schema and try unlock from healthy, unencrypted, unmanaged, stale and falsely matched devices. Refuse unhealthy or unknown under policy, show the member the source and fix, then accept a fresh corrected observation. Exercise a time-limited human override and source outage; expiry returns to policy and no Latchkey command silently modifies the MDM.

**LKY-028 — MSP and multi-company operations.** A provider console manages separate client companies with delegated administrators, policy templates, onboarding, health and adoption summaries and commercial records. Client keys, data, roles, audit and connector authorization remain isolated; viewing a fleet dashboard never grants access to client secrets. A template application previews the precise per-client changes, exceptions and delegated authority and reports each success and failure. Oncall owns paging and timelines; Latchkey reports who held the credential and verified rotation. Vault checkout is not a substitute for CyberArk and Teleport session recording.

**Acceptance evidence:** During a linked Oncall incident request AWS root checkout as an engineer, obtain the required human approval and deny a conflicting requester. Approve the actual provider-supported credential ceremony, check in or expire access and verify new login before marking rotation complete. Show the holder and timestamps on the incident timeline. Interrupt rotation, attempt an agent-created emergency override and an expired reveal: the blocker remains visible and no unauthorized access succeeds.

**LKY-024 — Credentials for AI agents without disclosure.** An agent gets a person, purpose, target and expiry-bound grant and opaque credential references. The MCP server returns references and permitted posture, never secret values; Browserbase or an owner-controlled Playwright broker injects into a controlled browser only after checking live grant and required phone approval. SDK and CLI injection puts secrets in an isolated authorized process, not the model's prompts, tool responses, screenshots, DOM snapshots, traces or logs. Redaction is defense in depth; isolation prevents the model from requesting secret-bearing DOM, network and process inspection. Target origin and redirect boundaries are enforced at each use; prompt injection cannot change them. The broker is a named trusted client with owner-held keys, subject to LKY-001 and LKY-057, not a vendor server that can unwrap every vault.

**Acceptance evidence:** Ask an agent to sign in to a test vendor portal via Browserbase and Playwright controlled brokers, approve on phone and inspect model context and tool responses, logs, screenshot and trace artifacts for password and TOTP absence. Audit shows agent, sponsor, target, approval and provider outcome. Inject a redirect, devtools and read-environment request and hostile portal instruction asking for another secret; deny them. Revoke the grant mid-session and prevent the next credential use.

**LKY-029 — Documented API, CLI, webhooks and MCP.** Publish a versioned REST API and OpenAPI and full CLI for users, groups, vaults, items as permitted client-encrypted data, policies, projects, events, reviews, imports and operation status. Pagination, stable IDs, conditional version updates, useful errors and rate limits support real administration without scraping UI. Signed security webhooks carry stable event IDs, minimal payloads, current scopes and rotation and revocation controls; document at-least-once delivery and idempotent consumer handling. MCP tools let sibling seeds and agents read allowed posture and request access by reference, with the same server-side policy and human gate as UI. No SDK or admin key bypasses the cryptographic and tenant boundary, reveals values by default or implies a machine can approve a sensitive grant.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through published API and CLI create and paginate a group, vault and project, perform a conditional update and reject stale writes, foreign IDs and expired credentials. Subscribe a signed webhook endpoint, fail and replay delivery and reconcile event IDs. Request finance-vault access through MCP as an external agent; only the vault owner's exact human approval grants it, and the request, decision and result appear in audit with no secret in the MCP payload.

**LKY-052 — Connector reliability and least-privilege credentials.** Every identity, lifecycle, posture, breach, CI, cloud-sync, scanner, SIEM, evidence and notification adapter exposes required scopes, actual authorized tenant and targets, last success, cursor and version, lag and current error. Connect, test and revoke paths use buyer-owned provider apps or narrowly scoped tokens kept client-encrypted; a disconnected integration cannot masquerade as healthy. Webhooks validate signatures, freshness, tenant and replay; polling has bounded checkpoints, backoff for rate limits and explicit blocked state. Operations have durable attempt IDs, leases and reconciled external outcomes across restart; ambiguous timeouts are observed and read back before another mutation. Revoking the connector stops the next provider use. A read-only scan cannot borrow write authority from another connector.

**Acceptance evidence:** Configure one adapter from every named integration family, inspect permissions and actual identity, then revoke scopes, rate-limit, reorder and replay events and interrupt a rotation after the provider accepted it but before acknowledgment. Restart and reconcile by provider reference without issuing a second destructive mutation. A forged webhook and cross-tenant cursor or expired connector is refused with no effects; diagnostics expose lag without printing tokens or values.

**LKY-053 — Provider-supported key management and consumer graph.** Maintain an explicit registry of supported key and account classes and official APIs or browser ceremonies for PostgreSQL, MySQL, AWS IAM, Stripe, Twilio, SendGrid, GitHub and Slack. Model identity ownership, issuing privileges, consumers, allowed overlap, quota and expiry, validation probe and revocation mechanism. Distinguish an AWS root password, IAM access key, GitHub PAT and app token, Slack OAuth token and signing secret rather than pretending they have one universal rotation endpoint. For unsupported API operations ship a real authorized browser and operator ceremony and retain a blocked owner step until completed; never invent an API or report a manual step executed. Consumers can report last-seen secret versions without disclosing values, and unidentified consumers remain a risk to resolve before destructive cutover.

**Acceptance evidence:** From the provider connection page select each named class, inspect supported operations and scopes and build a consumer graph linking app, CI and cloud sync. Exercise official-schema and sandbox create, validate, update and revoke flows and a required human dashboard ceremony. Introduce an unknown consumer and exhausted key quota; show the blocker and maintenance choice without revoking valid access or claiming universal no-downtime rotation.

**LKY-057 — Trusted fill-broker and runtime boundaries.** Deploy fill-only and agent credential brokers as separately enrolled owner-controlled trusted clients with keys scoped to the selected grants, not the full organization. Their security design isolates secret-handling from model and client-visible DOM, screenshots, network inspector, filesystem, crash dumps and telemetry. Brokers attest the configured binary and runtime identity and approved origin and action; owners can inspect and revoke them. A brokered sign-in proves credential delivery, not immunity to an authorized target application's account data exposure or theft of an authenticated session. Native applications and extensions cannot label a local plaintext DOM fill as cryptographic fill-only. General remote desktop, privileged-session routing and recording and endpoint management remain specialist products; this broker's scope is only credential delivery and verification.

**Acceptance evidence:** Enroll a fresh broker, give it one credential reference for one target, then try other vaults, origins, debug endpoints, crash reporting and model-visible snapshots while using the approved login. Prove secret absence at every requesting interface and scoped key absence from the control-plane dump; revoke and re-enroll and invalidate the old broker. Show a broker unavailable state instead of silently switching to local plaintext fill. Independently assess the isolation described by LKY-030.

### Agent remediation

**LKY-063 — Rotation agent changes and verifies the locks.** The agent reads authorized breach and leak findings, historic exposure, vault versions, provider capability and the declared consumer graph; it prepares an exact rotation and validation plan and carries the approved operation through API or brokered browser change, consumer propagation, fresh sign-in and old-credential retirement. It never receives the value, infers success from a save button or revokes old access before verification where overlap is supported. A named person approves critical bank, registrar, payroll and root changes, required single-phase maintenance and new grants; the agent cannot approve itself or escape the purpose and target grant. Unreachable consumers, CAPTCHA and MFA and missing rights pause with the exact human action needed. Already rotated items reconcile by provider evidence after restarts rather than blind repetition.

**Acceptance evidence:** Seed a breached shared credential with app, CI and cloud consumers; have the agent produce the evidence-backed plan, obtain required phone approval and complete the real provider-supported change. Confirm fresh login at every declared consumer and rejection of old access before the finding closes. Repeat with consumer failure, changed input, MFA handoff and interrupted acknowledgment; show paused or resumable state, no self-approval and no password in model, context and logs.

**LKY-064 — Offboarding agent closes the entire exposure set.** Using signed leaver events, current and prior grant history, usage and company ownership, the agent supervises suspension, cryptographic hand-back, service-owner reassignment and every necessary shared-credential rotation. It distinguishes accessible or reveal-capable, actually used and merely inferred exposures, includes the prior 90 days, and cites missing offline evidence. The named custodian and account owner approve sensitive transfers and critical rotations. Each exposure closes only with provider and consumer proof or a named unresolved blocker; a successfully suspended directory account is not proof old bank or vendor passwords stopped working. Notify the HR or IT source and responsible owner with minimum metadata while personal accounts remain independent.

**Acceptance evidence:** Offboard a person with an old removed finance grant, recent brokered use, private company item, sponsored agent and a family vault. Inspect a complete exposure set and human-approved hand-back, verify all attributable old credential access is rotated or revoked or explicitly blocked, and keep a failed critical rotation open. Replay leaver events and restore an old archive without reviving access; return correlated completion and blockers to Auth and Linen and preserve the personal vault.

**LKY-065 — Hygiene agent improves the actual vault state.** On authorized client-produced health findings, ownership, duplicate candidates and supported site-factor data, the agent proposes moving shared work items to the proper team vault, merging true duplicates, rotating weak, reused or breached values and enabling 2FA and passkeys. Each proposal cites exact versions, resulting recipients and measurable risk. A person approves changes to ownership and access, personal items, recovery factors and new passkeys; a member can dismiss or defer with a reason. The agent follows approved work and nudges the responsible member until a fresh observed provider state removes risk, retaining recovery codes only in the encrypted vault. It cannot inflate a score by deleting hard findings or weakening policy.

**Acceptance evidence:** Create duplicate shared logins across private-work and team vaults, one breached reused value and a supported site missing 2FA. Inspect proposed merge, move and enrollment with effective-rights preview, reject one and approve others. Verify no action on the rejected or private-personal item, successful provider login and recovery material custody after approved enrollment, and a health-score improvement explained by actual changed state rather than removed denominators.

**LKY-066 — Secrets agent removes exposure from working systems.** The agent reads only consented redacted scanner findings and source references from code and history, CI variables and logs, chat and files. It matches the exposed version, prepares encrypted vaulting, fresh scoped runtime references and a source change and PR replacing .env or hardcoded values. An authorized maintainer approves merge and deployment and source-owned message edits; critical rotation remains human-gated. The agent verifies deployed consumers fetch the new reference and exposed old keys are revoked, then checks the original working surface again. It preserves a residual-risk account of git history, logs and copies it cannot erase, without retaining their contents or sending plaintext secrets to an LLM.

**Acceptance evidence:** Expose a test AWS key in repository history, CI and a permitted Nook and Slack message. Let the agent open a real reviewable PR swapping plaintext for `latchkey run`/runtime references, approve and deploy it, and verify fresh application authentication and old-key rejection. Replace only source-owned permitted messages, show retained-history risks, and attempt an unapproved merge or other author's edit: no write occurs and the remaining owner action stays open.

**LKY-067 — Agent-access broker enforces the sponsoring person.** Using the task's named sponsor, approved purpose, current target, secret references and policy, an agent-access broker plans the minimum fill and injection, obtains the person's phone approval where required and returns only the permitted outcome. It supervises each credential use and expiration, reporting which agent acted for whom. No model message, target page, external tool or downstream agent can extend the grant, request an additional secret or reveal a value. Runtime credentials disappear when the authorized process and session ends; revocation stops future use and any remaining authenticated provider session is explicitly expired or tracked under its provider capability. Model context and outputs never become a covert export channel.

**Acceptance evidence:** Authorize a vendor-portal task with a one-use, target-bound grant, approve it on phone and complete login via the broker. Inspect attributed events and model and tool artifacts for secret absence. Attempt prompt-injected cross-target use, downstream delegation, DOM inspection and another use after expiry; refuse all. Revoke during execution and show provider session termination where supported or its precise unresolved expiry, without claiming a session was recalled merely by revoking the vault grant.

**LKY-068 — Remediation coordinator follows proof and human decisions.** From open findings, due access reviews, provider attempts, approvals, ticket handoffs and versioned policies, the coordinator prepares an owner-specific queue, chases missing decisions through approved notification channels and explains blockers with links to source evidence. It can draft approved low-risk tasks and run already delegated bounded actions, but sensitive grants and reveals, critical rotations, recovery-admin changes and exports always await the named person. Human rejection is remembered; overdue work escalates to a different authorized human, never to the model itself. It compares actual before and after posture and provider outcomes and leaves unavailable coverage unknown. Follow-up ends on verified effect, authorized cancellation or an explicit remaining blocker, not on a polished narrative.

**Acceptance evidence:** Give the coordinator a denied finance grant, an approved noncritical rotation, overdue review, failed connector and pending recovery-admin and export request. Verify evidence-cited owner queues and permitted reminders, real completion of only delegated work and no effect from denied or unapproved sensitive requests. Complete the review and repair the connector through public doors and observe reminders cease only after the real result, with no self-approval when the deadline passes.
