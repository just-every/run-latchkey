# Latchkey product contract

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Latchkey is the complete business password and secrets manager a US company buys instead of 1Password Business, Bitwarden, Keeper or LastPass Business, with the collaboration, privacy and developer depth buyers seek from Passbolt, Proton Pass for Business and Infisical. Dashlane Omnix, NordPass Business and Psono are included in the switching destination. It keeps employee logins, TOTP and passkeys, shared privileged credentials and machine secrets encrypted, usable on every device and accountable through grant, use, offboarding and verified rotation.

This is a build target, not a claim of shipped capability. No repository or code was available for judgment when the draft was written. The Status table records LKY-001 for the existing client-held CLI/API doors as done under the ct split of 22 October 2026; the independent Latchkey review is separately missing under LKY-076. The original desk IDs LKY-001–030 retain their meaning and numbering. Difficulty in encryption, provider apps, native clients, controlled browsers, certification or migration is engineering work in the named clauses, not a scope reduction. Naming and the friendly latchkey mark remain as specified by the desk; this document changes no brand, UI or homepage.

## Acceptance conventions

Every clause below names buyer behavior and a journey through a public browser, installed client, CLI, API or provider door. Browser journeys include realistic US-company data, success, refusal, empty, loading, expiry and failure states at **1440 and 390 CSS pixels**; native mobile, autofill and client requirements additionally use their actual platform APIs. Keyboard and assistive-technology operation is required. These are future acceptance obligations, not screenshots or tests claimed to exist today.

Use the actual migrated product and dedicated synthetic and test accounts. Fakes belong only at paid or network provider edges and follow the provider's official published schema and protocol; preserve request scope, signature, ordering, pagination, rate-limit and failure behavior. Such a fake establishes bounded adapter behavior, not a live connection. Native clients, end-to-end encryption, fill-only isolation, actual source exports and real-page fill coverage cannot be replaced by mock application code. Security review and certification clauses require their actual independent documents. Every journey includes denial of cross-tenant, expired and revoked authority, no secret leakage, a durable attributed result and the archive and erasure consequences of LKY-033 and LKY-034. No agent approves a person's decision.

Routes and command names describe required public doors and may be made concrete by implementation without changing the promised behavior. One-off fixes are shown by before and after evidence; permanent tests protect meaningful flows rather than pinning copy or document hashes.

## Published clauses

### Existing client-held vault foundation

**LKY-001 — Zero-knowledge vault and published security design.** Every item, secret value, TOTP seed, passkey private material, custom field, attachment and sensitive item metadata is encrypted on the client with per-vault AES-256-GCM keys and authenticated context. Each recipient gets only wrapped keys authorized for that vault; their account key derives from the unlock secret plus a device-held secret key. Password-authenticated sign-in uses a reviewed SRP or OPAQUE exchange, so the server never receives the password or an equivalent decryption secret. Key derivation, nonce generation, device enrollment, membership removal, recovery, SSO and rotation are specified in a published design. The operator and vendor staff cannot recover plaintext from server state, backups or logs. Owner-authorized clients, scanners and brokers decrypt only inside the explicitly trusted client boundary; they are not a vendor key escrow. The threat model describes malicious client and update and endpoint compromise honestly. Exact semantics (ct 22 October 2026): reviewed SRP or OPAQUE means OPAQUE (RFC 9807) or SRP-6a (RFC 5054) through a maintained implementation, never a home-grown variant; independent review of Latchkey’s use is LKY-076. Item types, doors and runtimes that do not yet exist inherit this same leakage journey as they land under LKY-002/003/004/008/009/017/018/024/029/030/032/033/034/051/057/067/070/072; no future door is claimed here.

**Acceptance evidence:** Through signup, create, share, edit, export and recovery and machine-runtime doors put unique synthetic secrets in every item type; inspect full server database dumps, backups, logs, error reports and network payloads for plaintext and derivable keys. Demonstrate authenticated-ciphertext and context tampering rejection and key separation across tenants. Publish the protocol and design.

**ct ruling — 25 October 2026: APPROVE WITH RIDERS; LKY-017 remains PARTIAL.** The present selected-export door is restricted to the current owner of each selected vault. Installation ownership, administration, recovery, billing, membership, read access or an agent credential alone does not confer this authority. “Company” selection must disclose its actual coverage and every omitted or uninspected vault; a caller-owned subset is not represented as a complete company export. The complete company-export target remains required without granting access to separately owned personal or family vaults.

Encrypted, versioned, documented open JSON is the selected-export default, protected with an independently buyer-held export key and openable without a live vendor account. Plaintext is denied by default. Enabling plaintext requires a separate explicit signed owner policy bound to installation/tenant, vault, owner and monotonically changing policy revision. This policy permits proposing plaintext exports; it is not consent to any export.

Each export additionally requires the named authorized human’s explicit approval of the exact reviewed plan and format. The plan identifies scope, source and policy revisions, content commitment, item/version/attachment counts, omissions, purpose, intended target and consequences. Approval binds that plan and format and has a finite expiry no later than five minutes after authorization. Changed scope, source, owner, authority, policy, format or expired approval requires renewed review. New export approvals require live authority and policy; offline opening of an already exported buyer-held file remains available.

The separately signed plaintext policy may remain effective until explicitly changed; it never substitutes for fresh exact-export approval. Policy revocation or changed authority refuses subsequent product-mediated export. Approval expiry does not expire, recall or erase a downloaded plaintext file. Plaintext is produced only within the explicitly trusted buyer client boundary, with a visible warning and private destination handling. Temporary copies are encrypted or erased after confirmed migration, with failures and outstanding recipient, provider, backup or device copies reported honestly. No remote-recall or verified-erasure claim covers copies outside the product’s control.

Attributed audit records policy changes, approval, refusal and export outcome using opaque scope identifiers, plan and policy references, format, counts, timestamps, expiry and reason/outcome codes. It contains no secret values, item contents, filenames, keys, tokens or plaintext policy payloads. An approval event is not proof that a file was produced or delivered. Following erasure, retained facts follow LKY-034’s non-identifying audit requirements.

Archives may retain historical policy revision references and approval facts but never carry an operative plaintext policy consent, export approval or other active installation authority. Restore starts with plaintext denied and requires destination authority and fresh policy/export decisions. Selected reimport verifies the signed content and reconciles every included version and attachment digest without silently dropping nonactive states or repairing history gaps. Unsupported cases refuse visibly. Full installation archive, restore, recovery and inactive-authority obligations remain LKY-033 and LKY-056; selected JSON is not their substitute. Later erasure journals fence recreation under LKY-034; an old file alone cannot establish subsequent erasure.

**LKY-065 — Hygiene agent improves the actual vault state.** On authorized client-produced health findings, ownership, duplicate candidates and supported site-factor data, the agent proposes moving shared work items to the proper team vault, merging true duplicates, rotating weak, reused or breached values and enabling 2FA and passkeys.
